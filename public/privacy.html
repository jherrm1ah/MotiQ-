Motiq privacy policy

Last updated 12 November 2026

Motiq is a car companion app: it keeps your car's health and service record, reads fault codes through an OBD-II adapter, answers car questions with AI, finds help nearby and runs a parts and services market. This page explains what Motiq stores, why, and what you can do about it.

What we store

  • Your account: your email, name, country, city, currency, distance unit and (if you add it) phone number.
  • Your cars: make, model, year, VIN and plate if you add them, odometer, service history, reminders, documents, fuel log, trips and OBD-II scans (fault codes and readings).
  • AI chats: questions you ask the AI mechanic, photos you send it, and its answers, so it remembers the conversation.
  • The market: your listings (with photos), messages with other people, and reports you make.
  • Quotes: jobs you ask mechanics to price (with your car and city) and the quotes you send or receive. Mechanics in your country can see open jobs, not your name or phone number.
  • Shared cars: who you share a car with. People you share a car with can see its history and add to it.
  • Motiq Pro: your plan, when it ends, and a record of each payment (amount, currency, reference). Card and bank details go to Paystack, never to us.
  • Errors: when the app crashes or something fails, the error message, the screen and the app version, so we can fix it.

Your cars, history, scans, documents, trips, fuel log and AI chats are private to your account. Your listings, with your name and city, are visible to other Motiq users; messages are visible only to the two people in the chat.

Location, microphone, camera and Bluetooth

  • Location is used on your phone to find places near you and to measure trips. It isn't stored on our servers, except the start and destination names of trips you save.
  • Microphone is used only while you're speaking a question, through your phone's speech recognition.
  • Camera and photos are used only for pictures you choose to send (to the AI, for a VIN, or for a listing).
  • Bluetooth connects to your OBD-II adapter. Motiq doesn't use Bluetooth to find where you are.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase hosts Motiq's database, sign-in and photo storage.
  • Anthropic (Claude) processes AI questions and photos to answer them.
  • OpenStreetMap services (Overpass, Nominatim, OSRM, map tiles) receive your approximate location or the address you type, to find places and routes.
  • NHTSA (the US road safety agency) receives a VIN or make/model/year when you look one up or check recalls.
  • Jamendo streams the music in Car Mode Radio.
  • Paystack takes payments for Motiq Pro.
  • Google, if you choose Continue with Google, confirms who you are and shares your name and email with us.

We don't sell your data and don't show ads.

Your choices

  • Edit or delete cars, services, reminders, documents, fill-ups, trips and listings at any time in the app. Deleting a car deletes its history.
  • Delete all your AI chats in Profile → Privacy & security.
  • Block people and report listings in the market.
  • To delete your account and everything in it, email us (address below) from the email you signed up with. We delete it within 30 days.

Safety

Motiq's advice is a guide, not a replacement for a qualified mechanic. For brakes, steering, airbags, smoke, fuel smell or overheating, stop and get help.
